About

I've been a software developer for going a decade, working my way through roles as a fullstack developer, project lead, tech lead, and architect — these days my main focus is backend development/architecture in C#. What drives me is curiosity: I like figuring out how things actually work, and I'm not afraid to try something and get it wrong along the way. I also love sharing what I learn with others, which is a big part of why I'm building this site — a place to write about the things I pick up along the way.
